Attestations

A stele is an upright stone slab, usually inscribed, raised to record something worth keeping: a law, a treaty, a name. This page is the most thoroughly attested object on the disc -- it lists Stele's own license, the license of every third-party crate compiled into this binary, and the full text of Terminus Font's license, all rendered through the very engine you are reading it with. No JavaScript wrote this page; no JavaScript could have (charter C3) -- it is plain markup, laid out and painted the same way any other document is.
