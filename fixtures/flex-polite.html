Flexbox, Rendered in Pixels

This two-column layout is real CSS flexbox: the article grows to fill the remaining width while the sidebar keeps a fixed 220px column. No JavaScript, no grid -- just flex-grow and a gap.

The header above uses justify-content: space-between to push the site title left and the navigation links right, with align-items: center keeping them aligned on one row despite their different font sizes.
